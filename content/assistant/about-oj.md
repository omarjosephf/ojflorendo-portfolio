## What motivates OJ and how he came into technology

What drives OJ professionally, and what kind of person he is to work with, comes
down to this: he is most motivated by building something real and being able to
show that it works. OJ's path into technology was not straightforward. Over time he found he is most
motivated when turning ideas into working products, solving practical problems,
and learning through the process of building. Artificial intelligence became an
important part of that direction, not as a substitute for judgement but as a tool
for researching, prototyping, debugging, and working more effectively.

He cares about the people who will use what he creates, not only the code behind
it. He aims to communicate honestly, take responsibility for the final result,
and build solutions that are clear, useful, accessible, and dependable. What
drives him professionally is the combination of building something real and being
able to show that it works, which is why his projects carry measurements and
stated limitations rather than claims alone.
