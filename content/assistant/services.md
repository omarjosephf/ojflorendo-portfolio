# Services OJ offers to clients, and how to hire him

## OJ's leading offer: a focused website or landing page

OJ's leading offer is a focused website or landing page for a small service
business. If you want a website built, this is the service to ask about.

It begins with understanding the offer, the intended visitor and the enquiry
journey. Work may include content structure and page priorities, responsive
design, front-end implementation, a clear contact or enquiry route, keyboard,
readability and mobile checks, page titles, descriptions and technical search
foundations, and agreed handover notes.

He works with Next.js, React, TypeScript, HTML and CSS, and builds to the same
accessibility and security standards used on his own platform. These services are
available for discussion now; individual scope and timing require OJ's agreement.

## Improving an existing website

OJ also considers focused improvements to an existing website. This suits a site
that broadly does its job but has a specific problem: confusing navigation,
difficult mobile layouts, or a page that makes the service hard to understand.

The work starts with a review of the relevant pages and the user journey, then an
agreed set of changes covering content structure, interface improvements,
responsive layout and accessibility fixes, and verification of what was agreed.
This is available now subject to a technical review: he does not promise that
every existing system is within scope.

## AI document assistants

An AI document assistant answers questions about an approved set of documents and
shows the sources behind each answer, so the people relying on it can check the
work. This is available now. If you want an assistant over your own documents,
this is the service to ask about.

It covers retrieval grounded in the approved documents, verification of every
quoted passage against its source, refusing questions the documents do not cover
rather than guessing, cost controls and rate limiting, an evaluation set that
measures the answers, and a management view over questions, answers and gaps in
the knowledge. Scope, stated limitations and handover notes are agreed as part of
the work.

OJ has built and deployed this twice. Cited is a deployed document assistant
measured against a committed evaluation set, and E.V — the assistant on this
website — is a retrieval-grounded assistant with cost controls, rate limiting and
verified citations. A management interface over questions, answers and knowledge
gaps has been built and works; its durable storage is still staging rather than
production, and OJ says so rather than implying otherwise.

What that evidence does and does not establish is worth keeping separate. Cited's
evaluation measures Cited on its own corpus; it is not a measurement of any other
organisation's documents. Each engagement is scoped to a defined document set and
a defined job, with document suitability reviewed first. Extending an assistant
beyond that agreed scope needs its own privacy review, running costs and support
arrangements.

OJ has experience delivering professional training in AI, Python and data, and
working in digital content and e-commerce operations. He has delivered training
professionally to UK and international groups, and the content and e-commerce
work is current experience from two active roles.

This remains relevant background and may be discussed for a specific
requirement — introductory Python applications, data organisation and analysis,
Microsoft Excel and Power BI support, training resources and technical
documentation, e-commerce content preparation, product presentation, social-media
content planning, or digital workflow organisation. Ask about a specific
requirement and OJ will confirm whether it fits.

His interest in marketing his own products must not be represented as proof that
he can sell revenue growth or run a managed marketing service.

## How to start a project with OJ, and how engagements are scoped

To start a project with OJ, use the contact section of this website or his
published professional email. A useful first message describes the business, the
intended users, the problem to solve, the current website if there is one, and
any known budget or timing constraints. A technical brief is not required.

He reviews each enquiry individually and will tell you honestly whether he is the
right person to help, including when he is not. Larger or unfamiliar projects may
begin with a discovery phase, a prototype, or one clearly defined module before
expanding. This is deliberate: it gives both sides evidence before either commits
to a larger scope.

## What OJ charges, and why prices are not published

OJ's prices, rates and fees are not published anywhere, and no price list,
day rate, hourly rate, or project cost is available on this website or through
this assistant. There are no fixed turnaround times or service-level commitments
either. Pricing depends on scope and is agreed per project after the requirement
is understood.

Any figure quoted before that conversation would be a guess. To get a real
number, describe the work through the contact section and OJ will respond with
pricing based on the actual scope.
