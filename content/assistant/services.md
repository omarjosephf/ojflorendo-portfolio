# Services OJ offers to clients, and how to hire him

## Website design and development services

OJ builds professional websites and interfaces for clients. If you want a
website built, this is the service to ask about. He builds responsive portfolio,
landing, and small-business websites designed around clear goals, strong
presentation, and reliable user journeys.

This service includes portfolio websites, landing pages, small-business
websites, front-end implementation, UX and interface improvements, accessibility
improvements, and technical SEO foundations. He works with Next.js, React,
TypeScript, HTML and CSS, and builds to the same accessibility and security
standards used on his own platform. He can improve an existing website as well as
build a new one.

## Python development, data and training services

OJ offers practical Python prototypes, data support, and structured training for
individuals, teams, and organisations. If you need someone to help your team
learn Python, or to work with your data, this is the service to ask about.

This service includes introductory Python applications, data organisation and
analysis, Microsoft Excel and Power BI support, professional training in AI,
Python, data and prompt engineering, and the production of training resources and
technical documentation. He has delivered this kind of training professionally to
UK and international groups, so training engagements are established work rather
than an untested offer.

## Digital content, e-commerce and operations services

OJ supports organisations that need clearer content, stronger product
presentation, or more organised digital operations. This is the service to ask
about for e-commerce, marketing content, and social media work.

It includes e-commerce content preparation, product photography and presentation,
listing and item descriptions, social-media content planning and scheduling,
marketing and training content, and digital workflow organisation. This is
current working experience rather than a side offering: he does this daily in two
active roles.

## AI development and AI prototype services

OJ builds carefully scoped AI prototypes for clients: focused AI-enabled
experiences designed around a defined purpose, with reviewed human output. If you
want AI work done, this is the service to ask about.

It includes portfolio and information assistants, document question-answering
assistants, prompt-based workflows, small AI-assisted automations, and knowledge
and research prototypes. He has built and deployed a production retrieval-based
document assistant with cost controls, rate limiting, verified citations, and an
evaluation harness, so this offer is backed by shipped work.

He is also open to collaboration on more experimental projects involving AI
assistants, agent workflows, business automations, browser extensions,
AI-supported knowledge tools, content-production systems, and small SaaS
prototypes.

## How to start a project with OJ, and how engagements are scoped

To start a project with OJ, use the contact section of this website and describe
what you are trying to achieve, the problem you want to solve, your preferred
timeline, and your available budget. He reviews each enquiry individually and
will tell you honestly whether he is the right person to help, including when he
is not.

OJ takes on clearly scoped small-to-medium projects, prototypes, training
engagements, and ongoing digital support. Larger or unfamiliar projects may begin
with a discovery phase, a prototype, or one clearly defined module before
expanding. This is deliberate: it gives both sides evidence before either commits
to a larger scope.

## What OJ charges, and why prices are not published

OJ's prices, rates and fees are not published anywhere, and no price list,
day rate, hourly rate, or project cost is available on this website or through
this assistant. Pricing depends on scope and is agreed per project after the
requirement is understood.

Any figure quoted before that conversation would be a guess. To get a real
number, describe the work through the contact section and OJ will respond with
pricing based on the actual scope.
